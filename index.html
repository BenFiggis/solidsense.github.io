a new deposits sensor for enhanced flow assurance

Take the guesswork out of pipeline deposits monitoring

SolidSense is a new type of pipeline sensor that directly detects hydrates, waxes and other solids the moment they start to form.
It is non-intrusive and installed inline, so it measures actual deposits in real time.
This allows operators to take corrective actions precisely when needed and verify their effect.

TECHNOLOGY

Developed at the University of Western Australia, the sensor is currently at TRL 4.
Worley is partnering with UWA for industry outreach and coordinating field trials.

Problem

Solid deposits in pipelines cause blockages, downtime and safety risks. Current approaches rely on predictive modelling, with limited visibility of actual deposits. This leads to overdesign, inefficient use of chemical inhibitors and conservative pigging.

Solution

A solid-state microwave resonator integrated into the pipe wall around the full circumference directly measures deposit thickness and distribution in real time. Installed as a short spool or flange wafer, the sensor is in-line, non-intrusive and robust.

  • Detection of hydrate, wax, asphaltene, scale and sand
  • Subsea flowlines and topside production
